Production

Liou Long Tai supplies woven fabrics. Our major products include 100% cotton, C/T, C/N, 100% special polyester and stretch fabrics, as well as various finishings such as suede finishing, moleskin finishing, water-proof and oil wax coating, or functional finishings on fabrics.

Our specialty is the unique hand feel on all of our innovative finished products. Our products are predominantly in outerwear and trousers' fabrics.
